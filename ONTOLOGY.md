# Alfred vocabulary

This descriptive map keeps portable concepts separate from normative rules. It contains no private instances, IDs, or assumed universal source of truth.

## Terms and relations

Projects contain artifacts and commitments. People hold roles and accountability. Goals describe intended change. Capacity describes available time, attention, or authority. A decision has criteria, options, owner, evidence, and outcome. Evidence supports or challenges a claim. An artifact records a draft, source, or deliverable. A handoff carries destination-safe context between settings.

Every record should declare source, owner, date, sensitivity, and status when those fields matter. Keep observation separate from a normative source. Deadlines can supersede current timing while retaining history. Preferences are contextual, dated, and expirable; they are not an inferred identity.

## Status distinctions

| Layer | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Recommendation disposition | A human or accountable party marked a recommendation accepted, rejected, deferred, or modified. |
| Execution state | Work is drafted, committed, pushed, or otherwise evidenced at a named boundary. |
| Publication state | A target audience can access a verified served revision. |
| Outcome acceptance | The accountable owner accepted the resulting outcome against stated criteria. |

“Accepted” always names the actor and object. Recommendation disposition, execution, publication, and outcome acceptance do not substitute for one another.

## Decision trace template

```text
Decision: [what is being decided]
Owner: [accountable human]
Criteria and constraints: [what matters]
Options: [credible choices]
Reason: [user's stated reason or observed evidence]
Expected outcome: [what should change]
Review trigger and date: [what would reopen it]
Actual result and counterevidence: [complete after review]
```

This is a portable template, not a new ledger, schema, runner, automation, or scheduled review claim. Use existing records only when authorized and declare their source owner.
